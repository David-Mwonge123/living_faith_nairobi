Who We Are

Living Faith Nairobi is a church that believes the same God who created the universe wants to have a personal relationship with you, and that he made a way to do so by grace through faith in his only son, the Lord Jesus Christ. We are committed to making disciples who will abide in Christ, be established in his Word, love one another and bear fruit. We believe that everyone at Living Faith Nairobi can have a part in God's great mission and are called to use their God-given gifts to edify and serve the body of Christ. By God's grace, we are trusting the Lord to be a soul-winning, life-equipping, and sending church that transforms our communities, city, and world with the gospel.
